# Compact renderer geometry contract

Primary sync/async/data relationships order the execution spine. Identity/secrets and operations components are grouped in visual planes within their existing parent scope. These presentation enclosures do not change CASG containment or assert new isolation controls. Existing parent boundaries retain at least 36px side/bottom padding and 44px header clearance. Long compound spines may wrap after three topological layers; cycles receive deterministic ordering rather than a false DAG claim.

The router allocates distinct source and destination ports, searches 10px gutter tracks, and rejects overlapping or insufficiently separated parallel runs. SVG elbows use bounded 6px quadratic curves; anchor dots and open arrowheads remain outside card interiors. Label placement checks other pills, 20px expanded card bounds and container border bands. If no label placement is found, the scene records a labelWarning; do not claim that output passed visual review. Inspect complex models before delivery.

Cards use soft role colors and human-readable subtitles. Supply optional component `role` for a custom subtitle. Colors identify category, not compliance or deployment status. All verbose controls and evidence remain in the inspector. Workbench chrome has one component/line legend and accessible segmented focus controls.

Matched visual preference testing against Archify has not been performed. Passing a fixture's geometric checks is not proof of universal publication quality.
